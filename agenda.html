Samantha Layton-Matthews

Training & development consultant · Researcher · Author

Samantha has designed and facilitated development programmes for more than 25 years, with a focus on leadership, communication, emotional intelligence and business writing. She spent ten years facilitating at the University of Johannesburg's School of Public Management, Governance and Public Policy, consults as a Senior Researcher to the University of Pretoria, and partners with the UK's Mobile Team Challenge to bring experiential learning into her sessions.
